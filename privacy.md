# Privacy policy

TV Launcher is a private app used on a single TV by its owner.

**What it accesses.** When the owner connects a Google account, the app requests read-only
access to YouTube (`youtube.readonly`) to read the list of subscribed channels.
It does not read, post or change anything else.

**Where data goes.** The list of channels is stored only on the TV. The access token
stays on the TV. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's and YouTube's own APIs.
There is no analytics, no advertising and no sharing with third parties.

**Removing access.** Access can be revoked at any time at
<https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>. Uninstalling the app deletes all stored data.

**Contact.** Via the GitHub account that publishes this page.
